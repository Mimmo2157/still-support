STILL / HELP

Italian, a little each day.

Still is a free, offline Italian-reading app with Persian translations. Its first story contains 30 everyday sentences.

Start your story

Tap Start Story. Three sentences become available each full day at your selected times. Choose distinct times during your waking hours in Settings. Starting late in the day may release fewer sentences on the first day.

Read and continue

Read the Italian sentence, tap Show Persian Meaning, and use Read to mark it. Released sentences remain in your archive even when you miss a notification. The story continues from your first unread sentence. The April 1 date is part of the fictional story, not today's date.

Reminders

Reminders are optional. Enable them in Settings and allow iOS notifications. Tapping a notification opens its sentence. If alerts do not appear, check notification permission and Focus settings on your iPhone. The app works without notifications.

Change the times

Time changes affect future sentences. Already released sentences remain available, and the app limits releases to three per local calendar day.

After the story

All released sentences remain available for review. This version includes one 30-sentence story; it does not promise an ongoing feed of new content.

Privacy and support

No account or online sync is required. Your progress is stored on your device. Read the privacy policy.
