STILL / PRIVACY

Your learning stays with you.

Still stores your story start date, sentence release times, reminder preferences, and read status locally on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share these records. It has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Optional notifications are scheduled locally by your iPhone with your permission. Turning reminders off in Still cancels its pending notifications. Your device's Focus and notification settings may silence or delay visible alerts.

Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. Removing the app removes local app data; manage backups separately in your device settings. If you previously used the focus-timer version, its old local records may remain in app storage until the app is removed; they are not transmitted.

Still does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos. The built-in Italian story and Persian translations work offline.

If you email support, your email provider transmits your message and address; support uses that message to respond. Do not include sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
